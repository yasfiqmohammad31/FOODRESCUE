## 5. Priority Issues

### [P1] Invalid Interactive Nesting (`<Link>` wrapping `<Button>` without `asChild`)
- **Why it matters**: Violates HTML5/WCAG interactive nesting rules (renders `<a href="..."><button>...</button></a>` in the DOM), causing screen reader announcement glitches and broken keyboard focus behavior.
- **Locations (10 occurrences)**: `components/layout/top-header.tsx:60`, `app/orders/page.tsx:94, 102, 114, 126`, `app/map/page.tsx:142`, `apps/merchant/src/app/page.tsx:62, 70`.
- **Fix**: Use `<Button asChild><Link href="...">Label</Link></Button>` or style the `<Link>` element directly with button utility classes.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable harden apps/consumer`

### [P1] Native Browser `alert()` used for Geolocation Permission & Errors
- **Why it matters**: Native `alert()` dialogs freeze JavaScript execution, cannot be styled with design system tokens, and break PWA immersion on mobile devices.
- **Locations**: `components/location-modal.tsx:50`, `app/map/page.tsx:36`, `app/notifications/page.tsx:32`.
- **Fix**: Replace native alerts with an inline non-blocking toast notification component or contextual banner.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable harden apps/consumer`

### [P1] Non-functional Policy & Help Modals in Profile View
- **Why it matters**: *"Standar Keamanan Pangan & Higienitas"* and *"Pusat Bantuan & Kebijakan Undo"* appear as clickable list items with chevrons, but clicking them triggers no modal, sheet, or navigation. First-time users seeking hygiene reassurance hit an inert dead end.
- **Locations**: `app/profile/page.tsx:101-120`.
- **Fix**: Implement bottom-sheet drawers or modal dialogs containing the food safety guarantee details and FAQ/undo policy explanation.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable clarify apps/consumer/src/app/profile/page.tsx`

### [P2] Missing ARIA Labels & Accessible Modal Dialog Semantics
- **Why it matters**: Search inputs, icon-only action buttons (copy, flashlight, filters), and the custom cancellation modal lack accessible labels and ARIA roles, hindering keyboard and screen reader navigation.
- **Locations**: `app/page.tsx:32` (search input), `app/undo/[id]/page.tsx:216` (cancellation modal `role="dialog"`), `app/voucher/[id]/page.tsx:218` (copy button `aria-label`).
- **Fix**: Add `aria-label`, `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-labelledby`, and `aria-live="polite"` to dynamic timers.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable audit apps/consumer`

### [P2] Simulated Static Coordinates in Map View
- **Why it matters**: Merchant markers are plotted using hardcoded CSS percentages over a static radial dot pattern. Users cannot pan across campus corridors, pinch-to-zoom, or view actual road routes to the store.
- **Locations**: `app/map/page.tsx:45-83`, `app/page.tsx:213-255`.
- **Fix**: Integrate a lightweight Leaflet or MapLibre GL map component with OpenStreetMap tiles, custom branded price pins, and auto-centering on the user's GPS radius.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable delight apps/consumer/src/app/map/page.tsx`

## 6. Persona Walkthroughs & Red Flags

### Alex (Power User / College Student Regular)
*Goal: Order cheap lunch between classes in <30 seconds.*
- 🚩 **Red Flag 1**: At Checkout, when Rescue Credit balance covers 100% of the total, the app does not auto-select Rescue Credit; Alex must manually find and tap the checkbox.
- 🚩 **Red Flag 2**: On the 60s Undo screen, Alex must click *"Konfirmasi Sekarang"* to skip the wait; keyboard `Enter` / `Esc` is not supported on desktop.

### Jordan (Confused First-Timer / Cautious Student)
*Goal: Wants cheap food but worries about hygiene, safety, and pickup procedures.*
- 🚩 **Red Flag 1**: Jordan visits Profile to read more about food safety, clicks *"Standar Keamanan Pangan & Higienitas"*, but nothing opens (inert placeholder).
- 🚩 **Red Flag 2**: If GPS permission is blocked in the browser, a native `alert()` appears rather than friendly contextual instructions on how to select a campus preset.

*Goal: Ordering one-handed while walking near the bakery.*
- 🚩 **Red Flag 1**: Share and Favorite buttons on [`/listing/[id]`](file:///home/mohammad-yasfiq/Documents/Development/College/food-rescue/apps/consumer/src/app/listing/%5Bid%5D/page.tsx) are in the top header, out of one-handed thumb reach.
